A Simple, Structured Path to Literacy

IntraAct delivers measurable results through consistent daily instruction, structured materials, and real-time progress monitoring.

Daily Instruction Model

40 Minutes a Day, 5 Days a Week

IntraAct is designed for consistent, structured daily instruction that fits naturally into classroom routines, intervention blocks, and summer programs.

  • 40-minute daily sessions build automaticity and fluency
  • 5 days per week ensures consistent practice and retention
  • Designed for classroom, intervention, and summer programs
  • Structured sequences keep instruction focused and efficient

Skill Development

The Five Pillars Essential to Reading

IntraAct systematically builds the five core skills identified by the Science of Reading, with a focus on automatic decoding as the bridge to comprehension.

Phonemic Awareness

Students develop the ability to hear and manipulate sounds.

Phonics / Decoding

Explicit, systematic instruction in letter-sound relationships.

Reading Fluency

Practice builds automaticity, speed, accuracy, and expression.

Vocabulary

Students expand word knowledge to support meaning.

Comprehension

Understanding develops as reading becomes automatic.

Mastery-Based Progression

IntraAct's Simple Literacy Readiness Assessment

A simple, step-by-step diagnostic that identifies early risk and continuously monitors progress through mastery-based assessment. Using explicit, systematic instruction, learners advance only after demonstrating mastery at each level—ensuring no gaps in foundational skills. Results are digitally tracked and used to place each student at the appropriate level. Students who reach the highest level typically demonstrate strong proficiency.

Total Time: 2–4 minutes
